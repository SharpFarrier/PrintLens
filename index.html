      Setup on Android
      1. Plug the TSC into the phone with a USB OTG cable and switch the printer on.
      2. If Android asks which app to open, dismiss it.
      3. Open PrintLens in Chrome, tap Connect printer, and pick the TSC.
      4. Tap the Chrome menu and choose Add to Home screen to use it like an app.
      Setup on Mac
      1. Connect the TSC by USB and open PrintLens in Chrome.
      2. Click Connect printer and pick the TSC.
      3. If connecting fails with the printer busy, quit any TSC or label software that is open, then unplug and replug the printer.
